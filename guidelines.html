Community guidelines

Pareshani ek jagah hai jahan koi keh sake: “Mujhe ek problem hai.” aur koi jawab de: “Main samajh sakta hoon.”

Be respectful

Dusron ki situation ko seriously lo. Mockery nahi.

Protect privacy

Apne ya kisi aur ke phone, address, school, passwords, OTP mat daalo.

No spam

Ads, repeated posts, meaningless text allow nahi.

No harassment

Threats, hate, bullying, ya kisi ko target karna mana hai.

No scams

Paise, personal data, ya fake opportunities maangna banned hai.

No illegal content

Illegal activities promote nahi karein.

No impersonation

Doosre insaan ban kar post na karein.

Health talks stay general

Yahan diagnosis nahi milta. Serious health issue ho to professional help lein.

Replies community members ki raaye hain, guaranteed professional advice nahi. Emergency mein local emergency services contact karein.
