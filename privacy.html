Privacy Policy

Pareshani aapki baat sunne ke liye bana hai. Hum privacy ko seriously lete hain, lekin “anonymous” ka matlab absolute invisibility nahi hai.

Jo information collect hoti hai

  • Account: name, optional username, email, hashed password (Supabase Auth)
  • Problems aur replies jo aap post karte hain
  • Helpful votes, saved posts, reports, notifications
  • Session cookies / local storage authentication ke liye

Anonymous posting

Anonymous mode mein aapka public naam nahi dikhta. Database mein ownership (user id) moderation, abuse-prevention aur “edit/delete own post” ke liye store ho sakti hai. Yeh absolute anonymity ka wada nahi hai.

Public content

Published problems aur replies public padhe ja sakte hain. Email publicly nahi dikhaya jata jab tak aap khud na choose karein.

Moderation

Reports review kiye ja sakte hain. Staff hidden/deleted content aur account status dekh sakte hain taaki community safe rahe.

Aapke rights

Aap apna profile edit kar sakte hain, apni posts delete kar sakte hain, aur account related sawaal contact page se bhej sakte hain.

Contact

Privacy sawaal ke liye contact form use karein.
